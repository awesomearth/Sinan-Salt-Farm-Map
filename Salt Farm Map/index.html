Sinan's Salt Fields

신안군 천일염전

Sinan-gun produces most of South Korea's solar sea salt. Its salt pans are spread across the islands of the county. The official record shows where the salt comes from, how much is made, and how much licensed pan now stands idle.

                06

                Method and sources

                How the figures on this page were collected, labelled and checked.

                Reported or calculated

                Every value shown is reported by a cited source, except changes since 2018, shares of the Sinan total and ranks. Those are calculated from two cited figures and labelled "calculated".

                Licensed or operating

                Licensed figures count all permitted salt pan or producers, idle ones included. Operating figures count only those producing that year. The two are never merged.

                Zero or missing

                Missing values are hatched or marked "no data" and never drawn as zero. In KOSIS tables "-" means 자료없음 (no data). A reported zero, where one exists, is drawn as an open marker.

                Units

                The KOSIS table counts producers (생산자수, persons). Other sources count businesses or sites (업체 / 개소). Charts keep the two apart and do not compare across them.
