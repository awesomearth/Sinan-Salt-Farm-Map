# Data gaps

This file lists every combination of region, decade and metric for which no sourced figure was found. Gaps are **not** filled by interpolation, estimation, or by sharing province totals out to counties. In the page, gaps show as "no data", never as zero.

Scope: Jeollanam-do and Jeonbuk State, at province and si/gun level. National figures and single-site figures (Gomso, Haeri) are context only and are not counted as coverage. "✓ years" lists the years that do have a figure, with the basis (T = total/licensed, O = operating, U = unspecified). "—" means no data for that decade.

Metrics: farm_count (salt-farm businesses or producers), area_ha (salt-pan area), production_t (solar salt output, tonnes).

## Gaps in the annual series

- **Jeollanam-do production:** annual 1990–2011; then 2013, 2016 (secondary), 2020–2022 and 2025. Missing: every year before 1990, plus 2012, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2023 and 2024.
- **Jeollanam-do area and farm counts:** only 2008 (S04), 2016 and 2020 (S09, secondary, basis not stated) and 2025 (S01/S06). Nothing before 2008.
- **Si/gun level, Jeonnam:** 2007 (production only), 2008, 2019 (area and sites only, S15) and 2023, plus Sinan every year 2018–2025. Suncheon appears only in 2019 and 2023. Before 2007 there is no county figure for any metric.
- **Jeonbuk State:** province figures exist only for 1999 and 2001, both from a single 2001 newspaper article (S10, secondary). The si/gun figures are Gunsan's farm count for 2001 (S10) and the 2019 sites and area for Gunsan, Buan and Gochang (S15). Gochang and Buan also have site-level figures for single salt farms (S11, S12). No Jeonbuk output figure after 2001 was found.
- **1950s–1980s:** no Jeolla province or si/gun figure was found for any metric. The only figures for these decades are national: 10,500 ha and 350,000 t in 1955, 12,000 ha in 1985 (S07). The page shows these as national context and leaves the Jeolla map as "no data" for these years.

## Known conflicts between sources (both values kept, neither adjusted)

- Jeollanam-do total salt-pan area, 2025: 2,316 ha (S01) vs 2,146 ha (S06 table 3, which repeats its operating figure).
- Sinan-gun total area, 2025: 1,869 ha (S01) vs 1,871 ha (S02 township total).
- National total area, 2025: 3,003 ha (S01) vs 3,001 ha (S06).
- Peak Jeonnam output: S06 calls 1994 (378 kt) the peak, then gives 382 kt for 2013.
- Sinan-gun 2019: S14 gives 2,744 ha licensed / 2,519.4 ha operating; S15 gives 2,612.2 / 2,555.9 ha. (Counts are not compared: S14 counts producers, S15 counts sites.)
- Sinan-gun 2023 operating area: 2,054 ha (S14) vs 2,055 ha (S03).
- Sinan-gun 2022 output: 203,244 t (S14) vs 203,234 t (S01).
- As published inside one source: Aphae-eup 2022 operating area 140.7 ha > licensed 115.3 ha (S14); Incheon Jung-gu 2019 operating 221.2 ha > licensed 34.4 ha (S15).
- Jeonbuk 2001: S10 says only three farms remained, all in Gunsan; S12 says the Haeri salt farm in Gochang still had about 2.14 km² of salt pans in 2002.
